# Policy review data

The global-indicators software reports on both policy and spatial indicators.

`gohsc-policy-indicator-checklist.xlsx` is the blank 1000 Cities Challenge policy
checklist (version 2, released 30 October 2025).  Download it, complete it for your
city, and save your completed copy in this folder (or anywhere under `process/data`),
then reference it from your study region configuration file's `policy_review` setting
to generate a Policy Indicators Report, or a combined Policy and Spatial Indicators
Report.

A completed example is provided with the bundled example region, at
`process/data/examples/ES_Las_Palmas_2025/policy/`.

A policy-only report can alternatively be generated online, without installing this
software, using [GHSCI Policy](https://policy.healthysustainablecities.org/) — either
by uploading a completed copy of this checklist, or by completing the equivalent
online form.  See the
[instructions](https://policy.healthysustainablecities.org/instructions.html).

Guidance on planning and conducting a policy analysis is in the
[policy indicators](https://github.com/healthysustainablecities/global-indicators/wiki/1.-Policy-Indicators)
section of the wiki.
