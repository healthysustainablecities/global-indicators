# 1. Policy Indicators

The policy indicators for the 1000 Cities Challenge assess the presence and quality of a wide range of urban and transport policies that contribute to creating healthy and sustainable urban environments.     

The 1000 Cities Challenge Policy Checklist version 2 was released on 30 October 2025, following participant feedback and consultation with our Policy Indicators Working Group. This update improves the user-friendliness of the checklist adding more detailed instructions, and updated formatting and wording.  The policy indicators have been re-ordered, but are otherwise unchanged.  Videos below show examples from the version 1 of the policy checklist. Indicator reports can still be generated from the earlier version using the corresponding earlier software release ([v4.9.1](https://github.com/healthysustainablecities/global-indicators/releases/tag/v4.9.1)). Further information is included in the policy FAQ under [validation and reporting](https://github.com/healthysustainablecities/global-indicators/wiki/9.-Frequently-Asked-Questions-(FAQ)#validation-and-reporting).

## GHSCI Policy

[GHSCI Policy](https://policy.healthysustainablecities.org/) is our online policy analysis and reporting tool.  Registered users can generate and customise Policy Indicators Reports online, without installing any software on their own computer, either by completing the provided online form or by uploading a completed 1000 Cities Challenge Policy Checklist Excel file.  Creating an account is free: use the **Create Account** option on the landing page.

We are planning new features and tools ([PolicyBridge](https://wun.ac.uk/wun/research/view/policybridge-policy-assessment-reporting-for-healthy-sustainable-cities/)), and welcome suggestions and feedback.

## Choosing your pathway

There are two ways to record your policy evidence, and two tools that can turn it into a report.  Whichever combination you choose, the directions below will help you get started with a policy analysis for your city.

**How you record the evidence**

| | Online form | Excel checklist |
|---|---|---|
| Where | [GHSCI Policy](https://policy.healthysustainablecities.org/) | The [policy checklist](https://github.com/healthysustainablecities/global-indicators/raw/refs/heads/main/process/data/policy_review/gohsc-policy-indicator-checklist.xlsx) workbook |
| Requires | A GHSCI Policy account; an internet connection | Excel or a compatible spreadsheet application; can be completed offline |
| Suits | Working directly in the browser, with no file to manage | Team-based data collection, working offline, or keeping a local record |

Both capture the same evidence and produce the same scored result.

**Which tool generates your report**

| Desired outcome | Tool to use |
|---|---|
| Policy Indicators Report | [GHSCI Policy](https://policy.healthysustainablecities.org/) (online form or Excel upload) |
| Policy Indicators Report, without needing an account or an internet connection | The [GHSCI software](https://healthysustainablecities.github.io/global-indicators/2.-Spatial-indicators-and-reporting-software), from a completed Excel checklist |
| Spatial Indicators Report | The [GHSCI software](https://healthysustainablecities.github.io/global-indicators/2.-Spatial-indicators-and-reporting-software) |
| Combined Policy and Spatial Indicators Report | The [GHSCI software](https://healthysustainablecities.github.io/global-indicators/2.-Spatial-indicators-and-reporting-software) |

GHSCI Policy is for policy analysis only.  If you also need spatial indicators, or a combined Policy and Spatial Indicators Report, use the GHSCI software described in the [next section](https://healthysustainablecities.github.io/global-indicators/2.-Spatial-indicators-and-reporting-software) of this wiki.

Full instructions for the online tool, including a field-by-field reference, guidance on what counts as a measurable target, and a pre-submission checklist, are at [policy.healthysustainablecities.org/instructions.html](https://policy.healthysustainablecities.org/instructions.html).

## To generate the policy indicators for your city
### 1. Download and review the 1000 Cities Challenge [policy checklist](https://github.com/healthysustainablecities/global-indicators/raw/refs/heads/main/process/data/policy_review/gohsc-policy-indicator-checklist.xlsx) tool. 

- The policy checklist includes an Instructions tab, which details the steps involved in collecting relevant policy details for your city.     
- If you plan to use the online form in GHSCI Policy instead, you do not need to download the workbook — but reviewing it is still a good way to see the full set of indicators before you begin.
- Watch this video for more information:

[![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0bbd0214-cff8-42ae-9e02-6f11c7fe77c4)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6AxWXu543nc&t=3s)

### 2. Develop a policy data collection plan.   

[![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1d80055e-e72e-4b4f-a65e-ea6296d3d8ed)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gswuhnSoOvs&t=1309s)

- Identify who will collect the data.
- Identify the relevant level(s) of government for your analysis:   
    - The checklist can be applied to any jurisdiction (e.g. local, metropolitan, regional government), as relevant to your city. However, the Global Observatory of Healthy and Sustainable Cities aims to include the whole metropolitan area of cities, wherever possible.   
    - If multiple levels of government are relevant for your city, include policies for all relevant levels of government in the same checklist.  

- Identify the relevant government sectors and institutions for urban and transport policies in your country and city.    

### 3. Search government websites, and policy repositories, and/or seek advice from policymakers to identify and retrieve relevant urban and transport policy/legislative documents for analysis.    

[![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bd12ab75-704f-48ac-9397-4e4f21037dc2)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zz59fhkjk5s&t=37s)   

### 4. Assess the retrieved policy documents using the checklist.      

[![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/92aa64df-d6de-4b97-8b96-4ea321f9bb73)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0e-vLdmrutQ&t=1683s)   

Record each relevant policy as a separate entry, whether you are working in the workbook or in the online form.  Base each assessment on the policy evidence you have recorded, rather than on a document's title or general subject matter.  Where an indicator is not relevant to your local context, record this and explain why.

### 5. Review and validate your completed checklist.

- Check that policies are formally adopted and current, that they apply to your study area, that the responsible government and government level are correct, and that source URLs or citations and page, section or clause references are complete.
- Check that measurable targets have been recorded accurately, and are distinguished from general aspirations.
- Where possible, have someone with local knowledge of your city (e.g. a policymaker or planner) review the checklist, and have a second reviewer check the assessments.
- The 1000 Cities Challenge team can also check your completed policy checklist and provide feedback — email it to [info@healthysustainablecities.org](mailto:info@healthysustainablecities.org).  You are welcome to do this before or after generating a draft report.

### 6. Generate your report.

**Online, using [GHSCI Policy](https://policy.healthysustainablecities.org/)**

- Sign in, then either submit the **New audit** online form, or drop your completed `.xlsx` checklist into the upload area — processing begins automatically.
- The report card shows a *Processing* status and updates to *Completed* when the PDF is ready.
- Read the draft carefully, checking that the city details, audit period, policy findings and local context are correctly represented.
- Use the ⚙️ settings and 🔄 regenerate controls to update report content, images, languages and the contextualised summary, and regenerate as many times as needed.
- Download the final report using the 📄 PDF button.

**Offline, using the [GHSCI software](https://healthysustainablecities.github.io/global-indicators/2.-Spatial-indicators-and-reporting-software)**

Use this pathway if you also want spatial indicators, if you would prefer not to create an account, or if you are working without reliable internet access.  From a completed Excel checklist the software can generate:

- A summary PDF
- Policy Indicators Report
- Combined Policy and Spatial Indicators Report (if also generating spatial indicators).

Reports are submitted by email whichever pathway you used.  An online submission pathway within GHSCI Policy is planned, but is not yet available.
